In this three-fold universe: Sattvic, Rajasic and Tamasic, when Tamasic nature gets disturbed, harmful atoms pervade everywhere in the sky, atmosphere, water, land… all over nature. Then it has a catastrophic effect on all living entities including humans. Due to this, we go through mental sufferings, natural miseries, and diseases such as epidemics  & pandemics, as well as wars, poverty and national and international instability. This is called Rudrakop.

Our rishi munis realized that the worship of Rudra eliminates rurdrakop, as rudra is the tamas form of super-consciousness energy. Also as the shiva lingam is as an emblem of rudra, performing abhishekam on shiva lingam as per vedic predestination attracts tamasic vibrations pervading in the atmosphere and counterbalances it, causing positive vibrations to prevail over the universe.

Among various worships of Rudra, the highest and most powerful is AtiRudram Mahayagam. By the leadership of his holiness samdhi-siddha Jagadguru Mahayogi Siddhababa, this extremely rare and immensely powerful Atirudram Mahayagya is taking place at Pashupatinath temple in Kathmandu Nepal  from July 18-26, 2022. 
Ati, meaning Ultimate, and Rudram, meaning destroyer of sorrow,  Ati-Rudram yagya is the ultimate destroyer of suffering. This  Vedic Shiva worship  brings about soul welfare, betterment and ending of global crises, and enhances human compassion to build a united sense of brotherhood and sisterhood around the world.
