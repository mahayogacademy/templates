Ashram 

A Spiritual Home for All
Situated on the banks of the sacred Kaushiki (Koshi) River, the Guru Ashram spans over 10 acres in Chataradham, Barahachetra, Nepal. Surrounded by hills, rivers, and natural beauty, the ashram offers the ideal environment for peace, reflection, and deep meditation.

Open to visitors and spiritual seekers from all walks of life, the Guru Ashram serves as a spiritual home, supporting sincere practice and quiet contemplation.

A Place of Transformation
The Ashram follows traditional Vedic principles while providing modern amenities for visiting seekers. Daily meditation sessions, satsangs, and seva opportunities create a complete spiritual immersion experience. Whether you're visiting for a day or staying for an extended retreat, the Ashram offers the space and support for spiritual practice.

The Three Pillars of Practice
The Ashram offers a complete spiritual environment centered on three essential pillars of inner growth:
Sumiran – Meditation and remembrance
Seva – Selfless service
Satsang – Spiritual discourse and guidance

The Guru Ashram is a rare place where seekers can engage in all three together in a supportive setting.

Ashram Life
Experience the rhythm of spiritual community living.

Sacred Living Spaces: Simple accommodations designed to support spiritual practice and inner contemplation.
Daily Satsang: Regular spiritual discourses and Q&A sessions with Gurudev and senior practitioners.
Seva Opportunities: Engage in selfless service as a spiritual practice, contributing to ashram life.
Sattvic Meals: Nourishing vegetarian meals prepared with love, following Ayurvedic principles.

Seva plays a central role in spiritual growth, cultivating humility and reducing ego through selfless action. Seekers can offer seva according to their interests, skills, abilities, and professional experience. All forms of seva are valued equally; no service is considered superior or inferior.

During Satsang, seekers gather to receive guidance and wisdom from Gurudev. This sacred space allows individuals to ask questions, reflect, and deepen understanding. Sitting in the presence of enlightened Jagadguru Mahayogi Siddhababa offers itself clarity and inner insight. Satsang is an essential support for sincere seekers, strengthening progress on the spiritual path.
