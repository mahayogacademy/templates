About
Unlock the timeless wisdom of Vedanta Philosophy designed to assist you in navigating the complexities of modern living. This course presents Vedanta in a practical and accessible way, helping students move beyond concepts to lived understanding.

This course is designed and taught by the enlightened Himalayan Yogi, His Holiness Jagadguru Mahayogi Siddhababa. It is based on the Vishishtadvaita School of Thought. 

Duration: 350 hours
Online Delivery: Zoom
Language of Instruction: Nepali

Course Outline:
Introduction (4 lectures): Begin your journey with an overview of Vedanta's main concepts and historical context.
Pancha Sanskar (9 lectures): Delve into the five purification rituals that are essential for spiritual growth.
Darsan and Sampradaya (8 lectures): Understand the different schools of thought and their unique perspectives.
Vairagya Prakarana (29 lectures): Explore the path of detachment and the steps to achieve true renunciation.
Mumukshu Prakarana (22 lectures): Learn about the qualities and practices of an earnest seeker of liberation.
Chit Prakarana (20 lectures): Gain insights into consciousness and the nature of the mind.
Achit Pakarana (10 lectures): Study the unconscious aspects of reality and their significance in Vedanta.
Brahma Prakarana (30 lectures): Examine the Supreme Reality, Brahman, and its implications for existence.
Sadhana Prakarana (72 lectures): Engage in practical exercises designed to advance your spiritual practice.
Prapti Faal (46 lectures): Discover the fruits of spiritual practice and the experiences of realization.
Prapti Birodhi (17 lectures): Identify and overcome obstacles that hinder spiritual attainment.

Total: 267 lectures
